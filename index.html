Kumar Shridhar

I am a second year PhD student at ETH Zürich in Switzerland, supervised by Prof. Mrinmaya Sachan and Nicholas Monath from Google. I work on understanding the reasoning capabilities of generative LLMs. I also work closely with Carl Allen (Post doc at ETH AI Center) and Manzil Zaheer (Research Scientist at Deepmind). Before that, I was a Masters student at the University of Kaiserslautern, Germany and worked with Prof. Marcus Liwicki on Bayesian CNNs with variational inference.

I spent my last summer interning at Amazon Alexa AI where I worked on setting constraints on the output space of a Neural Network. Before that, I was an NLP researcher at BotSupply, student assistant at DFKI (German Research Center for AI) and Insiders Technology GmbH and worked on estimating the uncertainites with the air quality station at European Center for Weather Forecasts. I also worked with Prof. Seiichi Uchida at Kyushu University on designing a probabilistic activation function during my visit to Uchida lab in Japan.
